Training with evidence.
Coaching with context.

PaceProof is an independent adaptive running-coaching application in private development. It is designed to connect an athlete's training activity and recovery data with personalised coaching, goal planning and approval-controlled structured workouts.

Training intelligence

Review activities, running volume, pacing and progress against goals using records from connected services.

Recovery context

Use available heart-rate, sleep and other recovery indicators to inform training discussions without medical diagnosis.

Structured workouts

Plan and review future sessions, with explicit user approval before publishing workouts to external platforms.

Garmin developer integration

PaceProof is currently evaluating Garmin Connect Activity, Health and Training APIs for a private authorised athlete integration. The service is not presently open for public sign-up.
