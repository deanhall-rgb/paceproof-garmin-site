Privacy policy

PaceProof is currently a privately tested running-coaching application operated by Dean Hall as an independent developer project. This notice describes the intended Garmin-linked testing workflow. No public athlete enrolment is offered through this informational site.

What data is used

With an athlete's authorisation, PaceProof may obtain activity dates, distances, durations, pace, heart rate, elevation, FIT activity details, scheduled workouts and selected available health and recovery measurements such as sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, stress and Body Battery. Not every connected service or device provides every field. PaceProof can also store training goals, preferences, approved plans and coach conversations.

Why and how data is processed

The purpose is to present training and recovery histories, analyse progress and trends, support personalised running coaching, and create, review and, only with user authorisation, publish structured workouts or plans. The private test uses a developer-controlled local application and database. The separately hosted public website contains no athlete account or Garmin data.

Services and third-party processing

Garmin Connect provides account authorisation and, when connected, activity/health data and workout-delivery services. Intervals.icu may be used as an optional training-data and workout-planning integration. PaceProof also offers AI-assisted coaching powered by OpenAI: when an athlete explicitly enables and uses that feature, selected training metrics, recovery context, goals and conversation content may be transmitted to OpenAI for generating coaching responses. These providers process information under their respective terms and privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts this public informational website, not the athlete training database. PaceProof does not sell athlete data or provide it to advertisers.

AI transparency and choice

AI coaching can use activity and recovery observations to generate explanations and recommendations, which may be inaccurate and should be reviewed by the athlete. The application will seek explicit opt-in before sending Garmin-derived personal data for AI processing. An athlete can decline or withdraw that permission; the non-AI data connection can be managed separately. Garmin-derived data and AI responses are not intentionally supplied as model-training datasets by PaceProof.

Data control, access and removal

Athletes can revoke Garmin sharing permissions through Garmin Connect. For private tests, contact the developer to request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of locally held personal data, or to withdraw permissions. Removal from backups and external providers may follow their respective deletion processes. Authorised external workout publication is separately confirmed before it occurs.

Security and retention

PaceProof's private test environment is designed to restrict access to locally stored athlete information and credentials. Test data is retained as necessary for the authorised evaluation, unless earlier deletion is requested, subject to applicable backup or legal requirements. This public informational website does not receive Garmin OAuth tokens or training data.

Contact

Changes

This notice will be updated before opening PaceProof to other athletes or introducing materially new integrations and hosting arrangements.

PaceProof is an independent developer project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin. This site is public information only; it does not accept athlete logins or health data.
